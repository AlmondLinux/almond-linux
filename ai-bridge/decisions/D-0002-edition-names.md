# D-0002 — Official edition names

- **Status:** accepted by the owner (answered in the Claude Code session, 2026-10-18)
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Proposed by:** ChatGPT (recommended option B), confirmed by the owner

## Context

Two versions of the names were in use: an early pair ("A All Large Distro Model" / "A All Large Linux Distro Model") and the owner's 2026-10-14 redefinition used in this repository.

## Decision

- **AALDM — A All Little Distribution Model**: the light edition for older and low-power 64-bit PCs.
- **AALLDM — A All Large Linux Distribution Model**: the large, modern edition for newer PCs.

## Reason

"Little" and "Large" tell the two editions apart; the early pair started both with "Large".

## Alternatives considered

The early pair (both "Large", "Distro"), and option B with "Distro" instead of "Distribution".

## Consequences

The repository already uses these names. The owner's Obsidian notes and any ChatGPT prompts that say "Distro" or "A All Large Distro Model" for AALDM are out of date.
