# D-0003 — AALLDM visual design baseline approved

- **Status:** accepted by the owner (answered in the Claude Code session, 2026-10-18)
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Proposed by:** owner (design reviewed with ChatGPT; ChatGPT reports the owner said "All done" after the final images)

## Context

The owner's notes of 2026-10-15 recorded AALLDM as "ongoing design". The design review was finished afterwards in ChatGPT.

## Decision

The final **Almond Fusion** visual design (based on "Design 03 — Almond Custom") is the approved visual baseline for AALLDM: graphite and charcoal with restrained gold and silver accents, a right-side Almond Glass application bar, optional widgets, Almond Spaces, a distinct modern wallpaper, and the official logo.

## Reason

Owner's decision.

## Alternatives considered

Earlier AALLDM images, which looked too similar to AALDM.

## Consequences

- AALLDM is **DESIGNED**. No AALLDM software exists; nothing is implemented or tested.
- The final mockup images are not in this repository yet. They should be added (for example `branding/mockups/aalldm/`) so agents can compare against them.
- Glass translucency, widgets and the Medium/High performance modes remain AALLDM features; AALDM's flat, no-transparency rules are unchanged.
