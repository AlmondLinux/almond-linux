# Code of Conduct

Almond Linux wants to be a project where beginners and experienced people both feel welcome to ask, test and contribute.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Be honest about what you know, what you tested and what you did not.
- Accept a "no" on a proposal gracefully; explain your own "no" with reasons.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, age, ability or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a moderator's request to stop.

## Where it applies

In this project's repositories, issues, pull requests and any other space run by Almond Linux, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

There is no private email channel yet. For serious problems (harassment, threats, abuse), use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, issue or profile: those reports go to GitHub's own staff. For anything else, you can open an issue. Keep in mind that issues are public.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaks this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does not stop, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.
